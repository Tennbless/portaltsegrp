Welcome to the TSEGroup CGL Communication Portal

This portal is a dedicated space for authorized personnel to receive official communications, stay informed, and conduct company business professionally.

Messages are private communications intended only for their designated recipients. The portal is designed to restrict access to each message to its intended recipients, subject to authorized system administration and applicable legal requirements.

Your sign-in information is private and must not be shared with anyone. Use only your own authorized account. If misuse of your account or the portal is detected, your access will be revoked.

Whether communicating with the Office of the CEO, administration, leadership, or colleagues, treat everyone with dignity, courtesy, and respect. Keep communications professional, constructive, accurate, and relevant to company business. Thank you for helping maintain a secure, respectful, and trustworthy communication environment.

Confidentiality, Privacy, and Responsible Use

Communications and attachments shared through this portal are intended solely for their authorized recipients and legitimate TSEGroup CGL business purposes.

Do not access communications that are not intended for you. Do not forward, copy, screenshot, publish, distribute, or use confidential information outside authorized business activities without appropriate authorization, except where disclosure is required or protected by law.

Respect the privacy, dignity, and rights of others. Do not use this portal for harassment, discrimination, threats, personal disputes, unauthorized solicitation, or other inappropriate or non-business purposes.

If you receive a message in error or notice a privacy or security concern, notify the administrator promptly and do not further share or use its contents.

Your sign-in information is private and must not be shared with anyone. Use only your own authorized account. If misuse of your account or the portal is detected, your access will be revoked.
